# Trailer · 1 · Idea

**One line:** tools flash past and expire; four verbs, one loop and one purpose stay.

The talk argues that what you learn about AI should not expire with this year's tools. The trailer acts that out in
53 seconds: a wall of product names gets struck through on the beat, a box opens and contains only four verbs
(Bash · Read · Edit · Write), the verbs close into a feedback loop, and the thirteen keywords of the talk land one per
beat. It slows down once, for the patient's phone with five questions, then accelerates through the ten
prescriptions, and ends on the only thing that does not expire: the purpose.

- **Audience:** physicians and students at Taipei Medical University, seen on a phone or projected before the talk.
- **Feeling:** clinical, exact, a little funny (the Codeximab drug label), then quiet at the end.
- **Constraint (house style of `htlin222/mograph-library`):** black and white only; every frame is a pure function of
  time `frame(ctx, t)`; no footage, no images, no GUI animation software; the score and voice are synthesized in code;
  loudness −14 LUFS; 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps.
- **Not in it:** logos of real products as images, stock footage, colour, gradients, glow.

## Beats of the story

1. *Every tool expires.* Product names struck through, one per tick.
2. *Open the box.* An isometric box draws itself; the lid lifts.
3. *Four verbs.* B · R · E · W, one per two beats, then faster.
4. *The loop.* The verbs close into a ring; the drop starts.
5. *Thirteen words.* /Learning … /Telos, a cut on every beat, with live diagrams between them.
6. *Break.* The phone. Five questions. Two light up.
7. *Ten prescriptions.* Rx 1 … Rx 10 at two beats each, then the 78.6% stat counts up.
8. *Searchlight.* A beam sweeps the dark and finds three words: presence, responsibility, company.
9. *Title.* AI Agents in Clinical Medicine — What does not expire.
